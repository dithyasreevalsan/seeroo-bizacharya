Business Plan Workshop for First-Time Founders

One hands-on day. Bring an idea, leave with a structured business plan, first financial projections and a clear next step.

  • DateSunday, 12 Oct 2026
  • Time9:30 AM – 4:30 PM
  • VenueAthikavil Complex, Urakam, Thrissur
  • OrganizerBizacharya Consulting Pvt. Ltd.

About the event

A working session, not a lecture

A hands-on, one-day workshop for aspiring and first-time founders across Kerala. Bring your business idea and leave with a structured plan: a clear business model, a first set of financial projections and an understanding of what a bank or investor expects to see.

What you will cover

  • 01Validating your idea and identifying the right customer segment
  • 02Building a simple business model and pricing logic
  • 03Preparing basic financial projections and a project report outline
  • 04Choosing a legal structure and understanding first-year compliance
  • 05Q&A and one-to-one feedback from Bizacharya mentors

Who should attend

  • Aspiring entrepreneurs
  • Students
  • Working professionals planning a venture
  • Early-stage founders who want to formalise their plan

Seats are limited to keep the session interactive.
