Entrepreneurship Opportunities

Build sustainable, community-driven businesses with funding access and mentorship support.

Rural Enterprises

Rural entrepreneurship creates employment, strengthens local economies, and unlocks opportunities within communities. At Bizacharya, we help aspiring entrepreneurs across Kerala identify profitable rural business opportunities and transform them into sustainable enterprises.

Whether you're planning a manufacturing, service, retail, dairy, handicraft, cottage industry, or community-based business, our team provides practical guidance from planning to growth.

What we do

Our Services

  1. Rural business opportunity identification
  2. Self-Help Group (SHG) business mentoring
  3. Cottage industry and handicraft business support
  4. Business registration and compliance
  5. Funding readiness and government scheme guidance
  6. Branding and digital marketing
  7. Market linkage and distribution support
  8. Business growth consulting
